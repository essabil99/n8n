Connect ChatGPT to n8n

Use your ChatGPT plan for n8n Assistant requests. Your plan limits apply. This connection also uses your plan allowance when n8n runs a request.

Checking the connection…

This connection applies to the entire n8n instance. Users with Assistant access share its ChatGPT plan usage. Create your n8n owner account first.

OpenAI currently documents this app capability for eligible Plus and Pro users. Successful browser sign-in must be followed by a saved connection and verified model access.
